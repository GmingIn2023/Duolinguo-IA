# Surface brief — Gusgus app (landing = Persuade, everything else = Operate)

Scope: `/` (Persuade), `/onboarding`, `/learn`, `/lesson/*`, `/review`, `/tracks`, `/profile` (Operate).
Direction pinned by the user: "Éditorial-objet". No concept-seed roll (pinned direction beats the roll). Code-led (no image generation available).

## Direction contract
THESIS: Learning AI as handling physical objects. Every lesson is a tile you can pick up; progress is a stack of tiles, not a bar of dots. Refuses the category default of round bubbles on a winding path and the SaaS card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Cool paper grey ground (#F1F1EE), white tiles with a visible pressed edge (a 4–6px solid underside in a darker step of the tile colour, no blur), true black ink, one signature colour per track used as flat fields: Bird canary, Gecko leaf, Fox vermilion. Display type: Archivo expanded/black, enormous; UI/body: Geist.

STORY: Visitor sees three animal tiles and a giant headline, understands "three ways in, short lessons", picks one in under ten seconds. In the app they always see where they are on the tile column, what's next, and what's due for review.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Landing — headline at ~9vw spanning the left 7 columns, three track tiles stacked/offset on the right at real scale, black pill "Commencer" bottom-left of the headline. App — left rail nav (desktop) / bottom bar (mobile), centre column = track tile column, right column = today panel (XP, streak, review due).

FORM: Pinned direction (user choice), position 1. Seed key: none (pinned).
Signature interaction: tiles physically press (edge collapses on :active) and the lesson-complete screen reveals the XP figure with a masked line reveal in the display face.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
